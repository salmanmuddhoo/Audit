In this short explainer we walk through five questions that give any manager a fast, honest read on the state of their internal controls.

## The five questions

1. **Can one person complete a transaction from start to finish?** If yes, you have a segregation of duties gap, and the biggest fraud exposure in the business.
2. **Do approvals happen before or after the money moves?** Approvals that happen after the event are records, not controls.
3. **When was the last time a reconciliation found something?** A reconciliation that never finds anything is probably not being performed properly.
4. **Who can change master data?** Supplier bank details, customer credit limits and payroll records are where most damage happens.
5. **What would tell you something has gone wrong this week?** If the honest answer is "the year-end audit", you need detective controls.

## What to do with the answers

Write them down, process by process. Where the answer is uncomfortable, that is your remediation list. Most gaps can be closed with a change in who does what, not with new systems.
