Compliance failures rarely happen because nobody cared. They happen because an obligation was never written down, never assigned, or never checked. This 20-point checklist tests the four things every compliance system needs.

## How to use it

Answer each question honestly with **Yes**, **Partly** or **No**. Any "No" is a gap; any "Partly" deserves a closer look. The checklist takes 20 minutes and works for organisations of any size.

## The four areas

### 1. Identify
Do you have a complete list of the laws, regulations, licences, permits and contractual obligations that apply to you, and is it reviewed when the business or the law changes?

### 2. Assign
Does every obligation have a named owner with the authority and time to meet it? Do owners know they own it?

### 3. Evidence
Could you show an inspector, auditor or investor the evidence that each obligation is met, within a day, without a scramble?

### 4. Monitor and report
Is there a calendar of recurring deadlines, a routine check that they are met, and a report to management or the board on compliance status and breaches?

## After the checklist

If you scored mostly "Yes", formalise what you have into a compliance register and calendar so it survives staff changes. If you scored several "No" answers, a compliance health check can help you build the register and the routines in a few weeks.
